How this is built in Power BI

Star schema: Dim_Supplier, Dim_Certification, Dim_Material, Dim_Date with facts Fact_CertRecord (expiry, status), Fact_Inspection, Fact_PO_Receipt and Fact_LotTrace. Power Query cleans and merges the supplier Excel register, ERP purchase orders and the inspection log. DAX measures cover the weighted score, days-to-expiry buckets and documentation completeness. A scheduled refresh plus a data-driven subscription emails the exceptions list every Monday.
