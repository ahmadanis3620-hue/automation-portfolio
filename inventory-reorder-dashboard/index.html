How the numbers are calculated

For each SKU, demand rate d and its variability σd come from the last 56 days of sales. Supplier lead time L has its own variability σL.

Safety stock = z × √(L·σd² + d²·σL²), where z is set by the service level (95% → 1.645). It covers both demand spikes and late deliveries.
Reorder point = d × L + safety stock.
Order quantity = the larger of EOQ (√(2·annual demand·order cost / holding cost)) and enough to reach reorder point + d × review cycle, rounded up to whole supplier cases. Stock already on order counts toward the position.

The same logic runs as a plain Python script (reorder_engine.py), so it can run nightly against a Shopify, ERP or Google Sheets export and email the POs.
